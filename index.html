How we work

A Florida florist, honest about a perishable product

Hahadas LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000491793, filed in 2024, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

Three honest things. Flowers are a crop, so a named variety may not exist in a given week — we substitute for equal or greater value in a similar style, and we tell you that before taking the order. Delivery is a window, not a time, because weather and traffic are real. And lilies are toxic to cats: we will identify or substitute them on request, though where flowers are placed in a home with animals is yours to control.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will I get exactly the flowers in the photograph?

The palette and the feel, yes. A specific variety, not always — flowers are seasonal and what arrives varies week to week. We substitute for equal or greater value in a similar style, and we say so before you order rather than after.

Can you deliver at exactly 2pm?

We will give you a window and do our best within it. Anyone promising an exact time has not accounted for weather, traffic or a gate code that does not work.

My friend has cats. Is this safe?

Lilies are toxic to cats and are in a lot of standard arrangements. Tell us and we will leave them out — it is a simple substitution and worth asking about wherever you order.

How long will they last?

It depends on the flowers and on care after delivery — water, heat and position all matter, and Florida is hard on cut flowers. We include a care note and will tell you which stems are the short-lived ones.

Is the wedding deposit refundable?

No, on the schedule stated in your written quotation. We buy for your date well ahead, which is why the terms are in writing before you pay rather than mentioned later.

Can you do an outdoor wedding in July?

Yes, with the right flowers. Some varieties simply will not survive a Florida afternoon outdoors, and we will tell you which rather than letting them collapse during photographs.

Something to mark?

Tell us the colour, the budget and the occasion, and we will do the rest.
